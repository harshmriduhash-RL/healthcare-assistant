What CarePilot does

One assistant. Four specialists. You stay in charge.

Why CarePilot is different

AI that proposes. You that decides.

Most health AI demos let the model write directly to your data. CarePilot can't — the system architecture physically prevents any agent from saving changes without your explicit approval. Every action is logged with a full audit trail you can review anytime.

  • Structural human-in-the-loop — not just a prompt instruction
  • Full audit trace on every conversation turn
  • Input guardrails block diagnosis and prompt injection
  • Direct dashboard edits when you want full control — no AI middleman
